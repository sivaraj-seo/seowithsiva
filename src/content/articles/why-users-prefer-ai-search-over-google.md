## How Many People Actually Use AI Search?

AI search use is large and growing fast, but it sits alongside Google rather than replacing it. Both ChatGPT and Google's AI products now report user counts in the hundreds of millions to billions.

### How many people use ChatGPT?

ChatGPT reached 900 million weekly active users in February 2026, according to OpenAI's announcement reported by TechCrunch. That was up from 800 million in October 2025.

### How many people use Google's AI search features?

Google's AI Mode passed 1 billion monthly active users, Sundar Pichai said on Alphabet's [Q2 2026 earnings call](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/alphabet-earnings-q2-2026/) in July 2026. The Gemini app had 950 million monthly users.

Pichai also said AI Mode is driving "an incremental increase in Search queries overall."

### Is information-seeking now the main use of AI?

Yes. The [Reuters Institute](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/generative-ai-and-news-report-2025-how-people-think-about-ais-role-journalism-and-society) found weekly use of AI to get information rose from 11% to 24% in a year. Its October 2025 report states: "Information-seeking has become the primary use-case for AI."

That survey covered about 2,000 people in each of six countries: Argentina, Denmark, France, Japan, the UK, and the US.

## Why Do Users Prefer AI Search Over Google?

Users give the same few reasons across surveys. They want a direct answer, fewer clicks, less clutter, and the ability to ask follow-ups.

### AI summarizes the answer instantly

Speed of answer is the top reason. In the [Eight Oh Two](https://eightohtwo.com/2026-ai-search-behavior-study/) survey of 500 AI users, 62% said they choose AI because it summarizes instantly. The searcher reads one answer instead of comparing five pages.

### Users are tired of clicking through links

Forty percent of the same respondents said they are tired of clicking through multiple links. AI search does that comparison for them and returns a single response.

### Traditional results feel crowded with ads

Thirty-seven percent said they feel overwhelmed by ads and clutter in traditional results. An AI answer usually shows text and a few sources, with fewer competing elements on screen.

### AI handles research and summarizing tasks

Bain's December 2024 survey of 1,117 consumers found 68% of AI tool users use them to research and summarize information. That is the work a searcher once did by opening several tabs.

### Users can ask follow-up questions

AI search keeps context between questions, so a searcher can refine the answer without starting over. This is a product difference, not a survey finding; no study above measured it directly.

## Are People Leaving Google for AI Search?

No, not in large numbers. Most AI search happens alongside Google, and much of it happens inside Google's own AI features.

### What does the traffic data show?

Statcounter put Google at 99.61% and ChatGPT at 0.39% in September 2026. It bases this on over 3 billion monthly page views. Statcounter measures visits sent to websites. AI answers that send no clicks do not appear in it.

### Why do these numbers look contradictory?

Each source measures something different. Statcounter counts referral clicks. OpenAI and Google count users. Surveys count what people say they do. All three can be true at once.

| Source | What it measures | Figure |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| OpenAI (Feb 2026) | ChatGPT weekly users | 900 million |
| Google (Jul 2026) | AI Mode monthly users | Over 1 billion |
| Statcounter (Sep 2026) | Share of referral page views | Google 99.61%, ChatGPT 0.39% |
| Eight Oh Two (500 AI users) | Who starts searches with AI | 37% |
| Reuters Institute (2025, 6 countries) | Weekly AI use for information | 24% |

The pattern across sources: people use AI to get answers, and many of those answers never lead to a visit anywhere.

## How Does AI Search Change What Users Click?

AI answers reduce clicks to websites. People who see an AI summary click fewer links and end their search sooner.

### How much do AI summaries reduce clicks?

[Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) tracked 900 US adults' Google searches in March 2025. Users clicked a traditional result on 8% of visits with an AI summary, against 15% without one.

Only 1% of visits included a click on a link inside the AI summary.

### Do users stop searching after an AI answer?

Often, yes. In the same Pew study, users ended their session on 26% of visits with an AI summary. Without one, it was 16%.

### How common are zero-click results?

Bain found 80% of consumers rely on zero-click results at least 40% of the time. It estimated that this cuts organic web traffic by 15% to 25%.

## Do Users Trust AI Search Answers?

Partly. Half of people who see AI answers trust them, and most double-check important ones elsewhere.

### How many people trust AI answers?

The Reuters Institute found 50% of people who saw AI search answers said they trust them. That leaves half who do not, or are unsure.

### Do users verify AI answers?

Most do. In the Eight Oh Two survey, 85% said they double-check AI answers somewhere else. The survey does not say where, but traditional search is the obvious place.

### How often do users click AI sources?

Among people who saw AI answers, the Reuters Institute found that 33% always or often click through to the source links. Another 28% rarely or never do.

## What Does the Preference for AI Search Mean for Your Content?

Each reason users give points to a change in how pages should be written. The table below maps the evidence to the response.

| Why users prefer AI | Evidence | What to change on your pages |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Instant summary | 62% (Eight Oh Two) | Put a direct answer in the first 50 words of each section |
| Fewer clicks | 40% tired of clicking links | Make each section answer one question on its own |
| Less clutter | 37% overwhelmed by ads | Remove intrusive ads and pop-ups above the answer |
| Research and summarizing | 68% of AI tool users (Bain) | Add comparison tables and sourced statistics AI can lift |
| Trust gap | 85% double-check answers | Cite primary sources and show a named expert author |

### How do you adapt content for AI search?

1. **Answer first.** Open every section with a one- or two-sentence answer under 50 words.
2. **Write self-contained sections.** Each H2 and H3 should make sense if extracted alone.
3. **Add sourced numbers and quotes.** Name the source in the same sentence as the figure.
4. **Show who wrote it.** Use a real byline linked to an author page.
5. **Measure by surface.** [Track AI Overview](https://www.seowithsiva.com/blog/google-ai-mode-visibility-kpi/) impressions in Google and AI citations in Bing, alongside clicks.
6. **Audit the basics.** AI systems cannot cite pages they cannot crawl or index.
